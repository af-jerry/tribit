Your privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

1. Introduction

This policy explains how Tribit handles personal information when you visit this website or contact us about web development and digital services. It covers information we collect, how we use and share it, and choices available to you.

Tribit is the brand name used by this website and is operated from Ghana. The registered legal name of the responsible business has not been provided in the information available for this policy. Contact us at hello@tribit.dev with privacy questions.

2. Information We Collect

If you submit the project inquiry form, we collect the information you choose to provide:

  • Your full name and email address.
  • Your company or business name and phone number, if provided.
  • The project type you select.
  • Your project description, requirements, and any other details you include in the message.

The form does not ask for a budget or project timeline. Do not include sensitive personal information in the message unless it is necessary for your inquiry.

The website code does not add analytics, advertising pixels, or tracking identifiers. The hosting provider may create ordinary technical request logs, but the hosting configuration was not available for review, so the specific information collected in those logs cannot be confirmed.

3. How We Collect Information

Information is collected when you complete and submit the contact form or contact us by email. The contact form uses EmailJS to send the submitted fields to Tribit's inquiry email address. EmailJS is a third-party service involved in delivering the form submission.

The website does not currently offer an active newsletter signup. Its former newsletter form did not transmit or store submitted email addresses.

4. How We Use Information

We use inquiry information to respond to you, understand the project you are asking about, prepare a quotation or proposal, communicate about requested work, and provide services if you become a client. We may also use information where necessary to meet legal obligations or handle a dispute.

We do not use inquiry details for advertising, profiling, or unrelated marketing. We do not sell personal information.

5. Legal Basis for Processing

We handle personal information in accordance with applicable law, including Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843). When you ask us to discuss a project, we use the information needed to respond and take steps you request before a possible agreement. Where required, we may rely on your consent or another basis or exception permitted by applicable law, and we may process information to meet a legal obligation.

The appropriate basis depends on the circumstances. Consent is not treated as the only possible basis for every processing activity.

6. How We Share Information

We share information only as needed to handle your inquiry, operate the website, provide requested services, or comply with law. This may involve EmailJS for contact-form delivery, the website host, or service providers selected to deliver agreed work. Authorities may receive information when disclosure is legally required.

The site also loads Google Fonts, Font Awesome from cdnjs, and the EmailJS library from jsDelivr. These providers may receive technical connection information when your browser requests their resources. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

7. Data Storage and Security

Contact-form details are sent through EmailJS to the inquiry email configured for the site. The available website code does not show where the email inbox or provider stores messages, what safeguards are configured, or how long EmailJS retains any related data. We cannot make verified claims about encryption, backups, storage location, or specific security controls.

We take reasonable care when handling inquiries, but no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure. The business should confirm its hosting, email, and EmailJS settings and document the safeguards actually in use.

8. Data Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to an inquiry, manage any resulting client relationship, and meet applicable legal or contractual requirements. No fixed retention period or documented retention schedule was available for review. The business should set and apply a retention schedule for inquiry messages and related email records.

9. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website code does not set cookies for analytics or advertising. It uses browser local storage to remember your light or dark theme preference; that preference stays in your browser and is not submitted with the inquiry form. Third-party resources loaded by the site may have their own technical practices, which are governed by their providers' policies.

10. Third-Party Websites and Services

This website links to independent websites and services, including project demos and WhatsApp. Those services have their own privacy notices and practices. Tribit does not control how independent third parties handle information when you visit or use their services.

11. International Data Transfers

EmailJS and the external font and icon providers are third-party services. Their processing locations and the location of the email provider receiving inquiry messages have not been verified. Information may therefore be processed outside Ghana, depending on those providers' configurations. The business should confirm the relevant service locations and safeguards before making more specific statements.

12. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, you may be able to request access to personal information about you, ask for inaccurate information to be corrected, object to certain processing, or exercise other rights and remedies available under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843). Legal exceptions may apply.

To make a request, email hello@tribit.dev and describe what you need. We may ask for information reasonably necessary to verify and respond to your request.

13. Children's Privacy

Tribit's web development services and this website are directed to businesses and prospective clients, not children. If you believe a child has sent personal information through the website, contact us at hello@tribit.dev so we can review the matter.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy when our practices, website functionality, or legal requirements change. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last updated. Please review this page periodically for changes.

15. Contact Us

For questions about this policy, privacy requests, or concerns about information submitted to Tribit, email hello@tribit.dev.

16. Complaints and Regulatory Information

Ghana's Data Protection Commission is the statutory regulator established under the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843). If you are concerned about how your information has been handled, you may contact Tribit first or consult the Commission's official website for current guidance and complaint procedures.

This policy does not state or imply that Tribit is registered with the Commission. Registration status was not verified for this work.
