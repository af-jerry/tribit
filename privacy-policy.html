Your privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

1. Who handles your information

Tribit operates this website from Accra, Ghana, and handles personal information when you visit the site or contact us about web development and digital services. You can reach us at hello@tribit.dev.

2. What the contact form collects

To submit an inquiry, provide your full name, email address, project type, and a message describing your project. You can also share your company name and phone number.

The form requires your name, email, project type, and message. Company and phone are optional. You can email us directly if you prefer. Please include only details relevant to your request.

3. Why we use this information

We use your name and contact details to respond and follow up. Your project type, message, and any company details help us understand your needs and prepare a quote or proposal. If you become a client, we may use relevant correspondence to agree on and deliver the work. We may also use it to meet legal obligations or deal with disputes.

For project inquiries, we process the details needed to take the steps you request before a possible agreement. Ghana’s Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) governs this processing.

4. How EmailJS, Gmail, and Zoho Mail handle inquiries

When you submit the form, your browser sends the form fields to EmailJS. EmailJS formats the inquiry and uses Tribit’s connected Gmail account to send it to hello@tribit.dev. Zoho Mail receives the message and hosts the inbox where Tribit reads it. Your email address lets Tribit reply to you.

Successful submissions display a confirmation on the page. You may also receive an automatic acknowledgment at the email address you entered if the auto-reply is enabled in EmailJS.

These services process the information needed to send, deliver, and store messages. They may process it outside Ghana. If you email us directly, Zoho Mail receives your message without going through the website form. We may also disclose information to authorities when required by law.

5. How long information is retained

Under Act 843, we must limit retention to the time needed for the purpose we collected the information, unless longer retention is permitted by law. The relevant period depends on whether the inquiry is still open, leads to client work, or needs to be kept for a contract, legal obligation, or dispute.

Inquiries may remain in the Zoho inbox or the connected Gmail account. EmailJS may also keep a delivery history, depending on its account and template settings. For information about a specific inquiry, email hello@tribit.dev.

6. Cookies, analytics, and other tracking

The website itself uses no cookies, analytics scripts, advertising pixels, or other visitor tracking code. It saves your light or dark theme preference in your browser’s local storage. You can remove that preference by clearing the site’s stored data in your browser.

The site loads Google Fonts, Font Awesome from cdnjs, and the EmailJS script from jsDelivr. These services may receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address when your browser requests those files. The website host may also keep technical request logs.

7. Links to other sites

Links to services such as WhatsApp and to portfolio demos take you to sites with their own privacy policies.

8. Access, correction, and deletion requests

Under Act 843, you can ask whether Tribit holds personal information about you and request access to it. You can ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information, or request deletion where the Act provides for it, such as when Tribit no longer has authority to retain a record.

Email hello@tribit.dev and tell us what you want to access, correct, or delete. Include enough detail to help us locate the inquiry, such as the email address you used and the approximate date. We may ask for information needed to verify your identity.

9. Privacy questions and complaints

For a question or concern about your personal information, email hello@tribit.dev. You can also contact Ghana’s Data Protection Commission for guidance or to make a complaint.

10. Children’s privacy

Our website and services are intended for businesses and prospective clients. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email hello@tribit.dev so we can review it.

11. Changes to this policy

We will update the date at the top of this page when we revise the policy.
